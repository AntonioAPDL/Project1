Scientific contribution and component interpretation

The introduction distinguishes dynamic source correction within the quantile
state-space model from the separate synthesis across fitted quantiles and the
adapted computation. A compact comparison map explains intended model roles and
which contrasts are available. T0 and noTF are explicitly different. Structural
removals are interpreted as sensitivities under inherited fitting settings.

The saved-fit audit covers 45 original benchmark runs and 25 reduced runs, with
400 individual fits. Sixteen have recorded/reconstructed criterion satisfaction,
314 reach their iteration budgets without established joint convergence, and
70 lack sufficient recorded joint criteria. Of the 175 reduced-model fits,174
are budget-limited; all 35 full-reference fits are also budget-limited. Some weak
Gaussian fits meet their criteria. Thus fitting status alone does not separate
good and poor scores. The text reports the large forecast-location mismatch in
the May 2022 transfer-free median fit, and explicitly leaves unresolved how fully
converged, independently retuned reduced models would compare.

The historical unconditional convergence phrase is not treated as evidence. The
future status message now reports the actual stopping reason. A parsed-expression
comparison confirms every other R expression is identical: numerical loops,
stopping criteria, fitting policies and sampling behavior are unchanged.

External references and practical findings

Seasonal climatology, deterministic persistence, and an established GloFAS
truncated-normal EMOS comparator were computed from existing local data. The
fixed training rules use only eligible paired observations, respect product
families and retain 143--364 cases per lead. All 140 fits and 560 optimizer starts
converge, with inactive artificial bounds and maximum scaled projected gradient
5.99e-6. Four verified primary references support EMOS and forecast verification.

The three added methods supply 420 new per-time scores. The 1440 existing scores
are imported unchanged. The selected quantile model's pooled CRPSS is 0.68601
at 28 days and 0.58967 at 8 days, equivalent to 68.6% and 59.0% mean-score reductions
relative to seasonal climatology. EMOS has lower CRPS in May 2022 at both horizons;
raw NWS remains competitive at the shorter horizon; seasonal climatology beats
EMOS at November 2021. These exceptions are retained in Results and Discussion.

Limits of this completed implementation

The work supplies the requested comparators, diagnostics, skill context and
qualified interpretation. It does not establish independent optimality of every
reduced model. Remaining uncertainty about their convergence and best attainable
performance is visible in the article and clause checklist. This is a limitation
of the current evidence, not a new numerical replacement or an unreported claim
that the scientific question has been settled.
