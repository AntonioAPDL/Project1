What changed and why

The six sections now follow problem, data, model/computation, application and
evaluation, results, discussion. The opening explains hydrological and
meteorological uncertainty before connecting them to available information.
The data section distinguishes USGS target observations, retrospective source
channels, forecast ensembles and transfer covariates in one compact role table.
It states units, transformation, extraction/alignment and relevant product versions.

One scientific state-space formulation carries the main explanation. The stacked
state, dimensions and channel set needed by the priors remain in the main text;
full selector matrices, augmentation, covariance algebra and algorithms are linked
in appendices. The actual VB procedure leads the computation section. Software is
cited beside the combined timing/hardware description, replacing internal runtime
keys. The twelve transfer predictors, their scaling and the 34-state dimension
are explicit. Daily angular frequencies and the slow component's calendar units
are corrected in prose and in the component figure title, without changing fits.

Predictive synthesis explains anchor ordering, distributional shifts, blending
adjusted quantile functions, tail construction, rearrangement and random inverse-
CDF sampling. CRPS quadrature is described separately. Local notation collisions
and verified appendix transcription errors are repaired. The long-cycle caption
identifies posterior medians, matching the summary actually drawn.

The forecast illustration now precedes comparisons and supporting historical
interpretation. The reference specification is introduced before its illustration.
All original evidence is retained. The main model is readable before the technical
appendix is needed; all 45 original active labels are preserved.

Claim-specific citations replace the generic nine-reference cluster. Product
citations accompany the data descriptions. Verified bibliography repairs include
ERA5-Land/GloFAS sources, Duan's publication year, Cosgrove and Demargne author
lists, Demargne's final issue metadata, Barlow's author order, and name accents.
The source-specific verification notes and primary URLs are in evidence/
article_revision_notes.txt; publisher metadata for the two long author lists is
retained alongside it.

Seven context/synthesis panels were regenerated from copied publication inputs.
Their keys reproduce actual markers and line patterns, distinguish providers and
information roles in grayscale, and remain legible at the inclusion width.
The line is the predictive mean on the log1p scale; the ribbon is the pointwise
central 95% predictive interval from the unchanged complete predictive matrices.
Ten existing displayed sample traces are correctly identified. The origin marks T
and the first forecast is T+1. The final review also corrected a clipped January
date label and regenerated the family together. The separate component-title
render used compact summaries, giving eight changed manuscript figures overall.

UTF-8 input, unpadded figure/table counter definitions and disabled decimal digit
grouping fix the demonstrated typographical defects. The original five-decimal
table precision is retained. Caption spacing is controlled separately. The Wiley
class is unchanged. A placeholder unpublished 'How to cite' block is suppressed
in the manuscript, and hyperlink borders are hidden. Abstract/main abbreviations
and later local acronyms are defined consistently.
