Reviewer comment 1: investigation and proposed revision plan

Decision proposed for review

Undertake one integrated editorial revision of the manuscript's architecture,
explanations, notation, citations, figures and typesetting. The intended reader is
a statistical scientist familiar with regression and basic Bayesian ideas who
does not already know this hydrological application, the exAL model or the project
history. A successful revision lets that reader follow the problem, information
roles, model, computation, predictive output and results in that order.

This plan addresses major comment 1 only. The other substantive referee comments
remain separate work items. It does not propose changing fitted models, forecast
values, scoring definitions, parameter selection or empirical conclusions.

The article has not been edited during this investigation. The accompanying small
typography PDFs demonstrate fixes in isolation using the actual Wiley class.

Assessment of the referee's six examples

A. Opening and response-driven prose: confirmed.
   At line 76, information changes into uncertainty sources without an explicit
   logical bridge. The runtime field names at 246 have no useful software referent.
   Contrasts at 84 and repeated qualifications elsewhere refer implicitly to old
   objections. The repair is a connected explanation of the scientific task and
   direct statements of what the model does. Preserve necessary scientific
   distinctions, such as fitted-quantile uncertainty versus predictive uncertainty.

B. Order of definitions and motivations: justified, with two qualifications.
   The exAL definition at 105 legitimately needs a quantile-dependent admissible
   skewness interval. Its complete root formulas and augmentation representation
   are excessive before the scientific model is clear. Keep the domain in one
   sentence and move the algebra to a computational appendix.
   Line 343 already attributes the multi-year period to exploratory spectral
   analysis. The problem is an insufficiently documented selection rationale and
   the separation of rationale, equations and later interpretation, not complete
   absence of a rationale. Locate the original spectral diagnostic if available;
   otherwise describe the component as an exploratory specification without
   inventing a physical mechanism or selection evidence.

C. Citation coverage: confirmed imbalance, not absence of all data citations.
   The generic Bayesian sentence at 80 has nine references. Section 3 already
   cites several data sources and methods. Reassign references to specific claims,
   strengthen exact product citations and correct bibliography metadata. Do not
   add citations simply to equalize counts between sections.

D. Figure legends: confirmed.
   The current Figure 1 combines color, linetype and markers in its legend.
   Figure 8 and A1-A5 use solid color swatches even where the plotted series are
   dashed or marked. Some source pairs also share linetypes, so showing existing
   dashes in the legend alone would not fully solve the problem.

E. Accents, whitespace and decimal appearance: all reproduced.
   - The compiled PDF displays El NiÃśo and PeÃśa. The local build uses the 2017
     LaTeX format with no explicit UTF-8 input decoding. Adding inputenc with utf8
     in the isolated fixture restores Niño and Peña without changing fonts.
   - WileyNJD-v2.cls:530,532 and 3249-3250 include an en-space in the definitions
     of the figure/table numbers. The .aux file therefore stores labels such as
     {8\kern .5em }, producing (Figure 8 ). This is not fixed by deleting ordinary
     spaces from Figure~\ref commands. Override the number definitions in the
     manuscript layer for both main text and appendices; keep caption spacing
     separately controlled.
   - Table 4 means are correctly stored as -0.00198 etc. siunitx's default decimal
     digit grouping displays -0.001 98. Disable grouping while preserving the
     numerical values and five-decimal precision. The isolated fixture verifies
     that \sisetup{group-digits=false} fixes this.

F. Acronyms: confirmed inconsistencies.
   VB needs a conventional first-use definition; MCMC and MFVB are repeatedly
   expanded. NWM and NOAA PSL need consistent introduction. The climate-index
   paragraph defines many abbreviations that do not recur. Abstract and main text
   should define necessary abbreviations independently; this appropriate repetition
   must not be mistaken for inconsistent use within the main text.

The deeper organizational problems

1. Mathematical machinery precedes the scientific target. Line 105 immediately
   introduces bounds, a mixture and auxiliary functions; the response and source
   relationships follow at 111-153.
2. The model is displayed twice in the main text: expanded scientific equations
   at 113-149, then a compact construction at 155-206 with new states, selectors
   and block matrices. Keep one readable scientific formulation in the main text.
3. Contributions are listed with computation first at 82. Introduce source-aware
   correction first, predictive synthesis across quantiles second, and the inference
   adaptation supporting computation third. Preserve the existing scientific claims.
4. Posterior computation follows development history: MCMC first, then the actual
   reported VB method and a generic tutorial. Explain the reported fitting method
   first; move detailed MCMC and generic derivation to technical material.
5. Scores appear before data, while competing models and fitted quantiles are
   defined only at the start of results. Move the full design before the results.
6. The principal predictive illustration is separated from forecast tables by
   covariate coefficients and three historical-component figures. Bring the
   predictive illustration forward and subordinate historical decomposition.
7. Synthesis refers to combining source information and to constructing one
   distribution from separate quantile fits. Explain those as distinct stages.
8. Internal terms such as quantile lane, staged bundle, canonical factor, selected
   output authority, filename compatibility and horizon contract interrupt the
   scientific narrative. Use scientific terms; keep implementation identifiers in
   reproducibility documentation where needed.

Recommended article architecture

1. Introduction
   Five paragraph functions: forecasting need and uncertainty; information roles;
   relevant methodological background and focused gap; contributions in scientific
   order; application scope and roadmap. Rewrite as a continuous argument.

2. Forecasting setting and data
   2.1 Study site, response, measurement units and transformation.
   2.2 Information sources and roles, summarized in one compact table.
   2.3 Extraction, daily alignment and relevant product distinctions.
   Define forecast origin and show the current context figure here.
   Keep this a concise problem specification, not a retrieval manual.

3. Bayesian correction and predictive synthesis
   3.1 Modeling target and brief exAL likelihood: location as target quantile,
       scale, skewness and one sentence on admissible support.
   3.2 Source-aware observation equations and state evolution. Explain each
       component's role before displaying its equation.
   3.3 Prior structure and dynamic adaptation.
   3.4 Posterior computation: model-specific VB overview and practical workflow.
   3.5 Predictive synthesis across separately fitted quantile models.
   Place full augmentation and compact matrix construction in the appendix.

4. Application specification and evaluation methods
   4.1 Level, annual/semiannual and multi-year components: rationale then definitions.
   4.2 Transfer features and numerical settings, with a concise settings table.
   4.3 Origins, two forecast horizons, comparison variants and fitted quantiles.
   4.4 Scores and aggregation definitions, preserving their current computation.
   Move the current variant definitions from the results into a small mapping table.

5. Results
   5.1 Representative predictive output, explicitly identified as an illustration.
   5.2 Comparative results over 28 days and the common eight-day horizon.
   5.3 Quantile-specific results.
   5.4 Brief supporting interpretation, only where needed for the main argument.
   Historical-regime and long-cycle displays can move largely to supplementary
   material. Retain their useful content without making readers pass through it
   before seeing the delivered forecast.

6. Discussion and conclusions
   Connect the stated scientific task to the presented evidence, practical meaning
   and focused future work. Avoid repeating each coefficient sign and caption.

Appendices
   Compact state-space matrix construction and detailed notation; exAL augmentation;
   detailed priors and algorithms; supporting historical decomposition; parameter
   tables; remaining forecast-origin panels. Use stable labels during all moves.

This structure moves the essential information roles before the model while keeping
application-specific settings after the framework they instantiate. Moving the
entire existing application section ahead of methods would leave another dependency
problem because it contains model choices that have not yet been defined.

Proposed prose direction: examples for review

Opening paragraph:
"River-flow forecasts support flood preparedness, drought monitoring, reservoir
operations, and water-supply planning. Their uncertainty reflects both uncertain
meteorological forcing and imperfect descriptions of hydrological processes.
Precipitation forecasts provide uncertain future inputs, while hydrological models
introduce uncertainty through their structure, parameters, and evolving states.
Statistical post-processing uses available observations and forecast products to
construct predictive distributions that account for these sources."

Model overview:
"For each target quantile, a shared latent river-flow process combines a local
level, periodic components, and a covariate-driven adjustment. The USGS observation
channel measures this target process. Each external product has an additional
dynamic discrepancy representing systematic departures from the gauge series.
Retrospective products inform these discrepancies over the historical period, and
forecast ensemble members enter the corresponding source channels over the
forecast window. Separate quantile-specific fits are subsequently combined into
one monotone predictive distribution."

Runtime paragraph:
"The reported analysis uses the study workflow built around the exdqlm package.
Fitting the seven quantile-specific models in parallel and constructing the
predictive distributions required about two hours on the reference Linux server,
which has 64 cores and 503 GiB of RAM. This is a combined fitting-and-prediction
time; the available records do not support separate timings for those stages.
Runtime depends on the hardware, historical length and state dimension."

These examples specify the intended clarity and order. They are not yet manuscript
replacements. The final prose should cite the corresponding sources and preserve
all technical qualifications that affect the scientific meaning.

Acceptance criteria for completion

The manuscript is ready to mark comment 1 addressed when:
   1. A reader can identify the response, external channels, covariates, correction
      mechanism, fitted quantiles, synthesis output and evaluation layout without
      repository context or prior review correspondence.
   2. Each major object has a purpose before its technical definition; one main-text
      model formulation carries the scientific explanation.
   3. Terms, acronyms and symbols have a consistent meaning and first-use definition.
   4. Every material factual/method claim has an appropriate nearby source or a clear
      statement of study-specific evidence; citation clusters have a specific role.
   5. Figure and table references resolve, have no embedded trailing spacing, and
      remain linked correctly after renumbering. Main and appendix labels both pass.
   6. Accents render correctly and survive PDF text extraction; Table 4 decimals
      display continuously with unchanged values and precision.
   7. Every figure legend reproduces actual styles; source identities remain clear
      in grayscale; labels are readable at the final size; captions name actual
      visible statistics and interval types; axes and origin conventions agree.
   8. Numerical source files, fitted objects and scores are unchanged by the editorial
      revision; only presentation assets and their generated metadata change.
   9. LaTeX/BibTeX compilation, figure paths, output lineage and applicable existing
      repository checks pass. A human reading pass remains a required final check.
