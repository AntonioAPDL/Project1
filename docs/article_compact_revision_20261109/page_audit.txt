Physical layout diagnosis
The unmodified Wiley STIX1COL class uses 10bp main type and 13bp baselines. Several generated tables already use 8.5pt type with 8pt notes. Font and margin reduction is not an appropriate primary remedy. pdfinfo gives a 595.276 x782.362pt page. Typical body text spans approximately y57-y725. Running heads sit near y38.

1. Current p20 contains availability statements only. Approximately 425pt of potential body area remains below the final text. A clearer and shorter main body/statement block could eliminate this tail page, but a dedicated supplement should still begin cleanly on its own first page. Removing a clearpage alone is not a dependable net content reduction.
2. Current pp26 and28 are centered float pages containing respectively about261pt and217pt of table text. They are visibly sparse, especially p28. The explicit FloatBarrier immediately after the shape/scale tables drains a long table queue before historical figures. This barrier did not exist at baseline. Local float reordering/consolidation should be tested after decisions about which tables remain. Do not globally weaken placement constraints before inspecting the proof.
3. Both revisions contain three explicit clearpage commands (before appendix, between sampling and VB appendices, before bibliography). The new FloatBarrier is the additional hard float drain. The section-scoped placeins package also creates automatic barriers at section boundaries. Appendix A is one large section, so its subsection headings can precede the actual queued display by one or more pages.
4. The Wiley class sets floatpagefraction and dblfloatpagefraction to .95, topfraction .921, with24pt default float/text spacing. Combined with late barriers, multiple large floats and [p]-only figures, this explains delayed and subsequently isolated pages. Do not edit the journal class or reduce body typography. Prefer removing redundant displays, then using explicit logical multi-panel floats and preserving local order. Any limited float-placement intervention requires visual proof review.
5. Four remaining-origin forecasts occupy pp32-35, one per page. Each raster is1993x1568, rendered450pt wide and about354pt tall, plus a roughly110-word caption. Its tall aspect ratio includes a large repeated title, date subtitle, nine-entry legend and footer. The statistical panel itself could be rendered more economically with a common legend. Simply shrinking four complete raster images to fit a2x2 page risks unreadable labels. Conservative recommendation: two horizontal panels per supplementary page, same numeric layers, common legend, shared scale/interval definition, separate date identifiers. Four-to-two pages is a plausible design objective, not a verified saving until compiled.
6. Main Figures1-4 and Appendix FiguresA1-A6 are often given the same450pt width despite different roles/aspect ratios. Resize by purpose only after a legibility check. The dry and wet figures can share a caption/legend. The long-cycle panel is less central to the forecasting contribution and can remain available in the repository if omitted from the short supplement.
7. Main Table1 and Table2 reproduce short taxonomies already given in adjacent prose. Replacing each with a compact paragraph can save both display area and duplicated explanation. Their information roles and model labels must remain accessible before the mathematical model and results.
8. Full quantile Table6 is a genuine140-cell origin/probability result display, not suitable for deletion of all evidence. A compact main summary plus a full supplementary table is reasonable. A transfer saves MAIN pages but not combined pages; compacting its caption and avoiding repeated model-code notes yields genuine combined reduction.
9. Appendix A exact transformed-score, adjusted-score, physical-scale-score and per-origin tables have different purposes, but overlap in rows and notes. Keep the physical-scale and per-origin calibration evidence accessible. Prefer one concise score-sensitivity note plus a machine-readable complete table rather than every matrix rendered separately if the detailed matrix does not support a distinct claim. Do not count removal of repeated columns and simple relocation as independent savings.
10. Gamma and sigma tables span about one to one-and-a-half pages together, including the sparse final page. They do not enter a main quantitative inference beyond approximate parameter interpretation; omitting their typeset versions while preserving the machine-readable results is a reasonable candidate. Historical sampling algorithms similarly occupy two pages although the reported application uses VB. Retain a clear citation to the sampler implementation/derivation; preserve the VB algorithm and model definitions needed to assess the claimed computational method.
