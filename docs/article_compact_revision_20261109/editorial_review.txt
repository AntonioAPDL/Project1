DECISION

The previous plan is well directed but should not be implemented literally in
three respects. (1) The seven-row external comparison would leave source/transfer
and Gaussian comparisons without any main numerical display. Expand the SAME
main score/skill table to all 14 methods rather than adding a fourth table or
removing the compact quantile table. (2) Deleting historical sampling displays
requires transplanting the GIG convention and removing live algorithm references.
(3) Use 30--34 combined pages as a conservative design range; 28--32 is a
conditional stretch outcome. Article economy is the goal, not a numerical quota.

Preferred three main tables:
T1 All 14 methods, mean CRPS and climatology-relative skill, both horizons. Group
   rows into external references and Bayesian configurations; no new parameters,

T2 Four quantile configurations x seven target probabilities, pooled 28-day check

4. MINIMUM COMPUTATIONAL SI; A CRITICAL DELETION DEPENDENCY

The explicit GIG convention currently occurs in the introduction of Appendix C:
GIG(p,a,b) proportional to x^(p-1) exp[-(a*x+b/x)/2], x>0.
Deleting C without moving this convention leaves the VB augmentation update
undefined. Move it to the retained augmentation/VB preamble. Remove Alg3's
reference to Alg1 and Alg4's reference to Alg2. Remove the main promotional
reference to historical Algorithms1/2, preserving the VB-accuracy qualification.

Retain:
- augmentation domain and A/B/C definitions, v=sigma*u convention and positive
  truncated-normal variance convention;
- the full factorization, expected precision and precision-weighted offset;
- the GIG and truncated-normal parameter formulas, including update iteration
  ordering and required moments;
- joint channel scale/skewness Laplace mode/curvature and Delta formulas;
- source/member stacking and summation counts for the forecast contribution;
- selectors, active-state restrictions, transition mapping and covariance-prior
  dimensions/formula;
- initialization, stopping criteria, iteration-limit interpretation, historical
  versus forecast scope;
- the numerical synthesis/empirical-quantile conventions.

Standard Gaussian filtering/smoothing recursions can be stated once or cited,
but the custom expected precision/offset cannot be cited away. The required
smoothed cross-time moments must remain evaluable for the forecast innovation
covariance formula. Do not silently rename the historical-only display a complete
joint algorithm. Consolidation changes presentation, not the inference target,
factorization, optimizer, conventions or numeric settings.
