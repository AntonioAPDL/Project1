2. JOURNAL BASIS AND EDITORIAL STANDARD

The current indexed Environmetrics Author Guidelines advise no more than 40 total
initial-manuscript pages, including abstract, references, tables, figures and
legends, and encourage separate self-contained Supporting Information for
ancillary technical details and secondary displays. The same guidance permits
Free Format submission. Its detailed presentation section describes a 12-point,
double-spaced manuscript. Therefore the present publisher-style proof is not a
universal submission-page conversion. Check the final chosen submission format;
report both main and supporting-document lengths. The supplement needs its own
title, authors, definitions and cited-reference list.

Editorial decision rule for each paragraph or exhibit:
  Does it establish the problem/contribution, specify the method, define the
  evaluation, substantiate a finding, or qualify the interpretation?
Keep it in main if it is needed to follow that argument. Put detailed evidence in
Supporting Information when an informed reader can follow the main argument
without it. Retain optional inventories and illustrations in the repository.
Delete repeated explanation. Preserve the source and meaning of retained results.

Use one principal location for each definition and result. Introduce necessary
symbols before use. Keep the short local restatement that makes a table or figure
independently interpretable. Present a numerical result in a table and explain
its scientific implication in prose; do not narrate every cell. Compress repeated
qualifications without removing the qualification attached to the relevant claim.
