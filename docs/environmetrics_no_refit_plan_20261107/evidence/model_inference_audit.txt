COMMENT 5: EXACT REQUEST AND JUSTIFICATION
The referee questions (a) 51 GloFAS versus 7 NWS conditionally independent member observations and implied information weighting, and (b) use of a common retrospective/forecast discrepancy and scale specification without explicit lead dependence. The concern about the observation residual assumptions is justified. It should not be paraphrased as proof that member observations are marginally independent or that the entire forecast variance/state is frozen.
