2. The required deliverable

Deliver a polished article based on the current results, with:
- exact numerical settings and correct parameter-summary interpretation;
- explicit uncertainty and synthesis descriptions;
- distribution-score and sample-size analyses from saved predictions;
- quantile reliability, actual 95% predictive coverage, and discharge-scale
  verification, including unfavorable findings;
- a precise explanation of the member-level working likelihood and descriptive
  information about its inputs;
- generated artifacts, reproducibility records, tests and a rendered proof.

Complete reporting/assessment is distinct from testing a new model. The reviewer
ledger must retain the numerical VB/MCMC comparison and alternative-likelihood
experiments as later work. The article receives no placeholder result table or
internal account of the implementation campaign.

3. Fix the score and quantile policies before computing new results

Preserve the original quantile-grid score and its existing skill values. The
required new common comparison is exact ECDF CRPS of every saved finite predictive
representation, with exact-ECDF climatology scores on the same cases and scale
for its CRPSS. The frozen deterministic baseline grids are the reference
representations in this comparison. Analytic EMOS or a differently weighted
climatological distribution is not silently substituted midway through the work.

Add a separate iid-based pairwise-score sensitivity for the 30 stochastic AL/exAL
objects, 15 stochastic Gaussian objects, and ten raw physical-ensemble objects.
Gaussian sample sizes are 64 or 48, not 2,000. Their interpretation concerns the
implemented predictive generator, not an assertion of exact posterior inference.
Physical-member iid behavior is an explicit working assumption for this
sensitivity. No universal independence or fairness claim follows.

Deterministic climatology/EMOS grids and the persistence point mass get an
explicit not-applicable status in the adjusted-score column, never zero or an
unlabeled unadjusted replacement. Do not add an adjusted CRPSS column in this
phase. Exact-ECDF CRPSS already gives a coherent skill comparison for every method.
Do not regenerate draws, repeat Gaussian values, deduplicate members, or thin
everything to seven values to manufacture equal ensemble sizes.

Quantile definitions are role-specific:
- Preserve the existing HE4 target quantiles for the four principal competitors.
  They are the authoritative delivered values for those targeted diagnostics.
- The demonstrated type-8 reproduction applies to the selected multivariate
  figure family. Use type 8 for its displayed 95% ribbon and record that proof.
- For other stochastic model summaries newly derived from matrices, explicitly
  declare type 8 as a new diagnostic convention; do not claim it generated every
  earlier export.
- Use the empirical generalized inverse/type 1 for new raw-ensemble and
  deterministic-grid distribution quantiles. Persistence stays a point mass.
- Keep fitted latent locations, own-fit anchors and final synthesized quantiles
  as separate mathematical objects and separate record types.

For original-scale coverage and targeted losses, inverse-transform the declared
log-scale quantiles/limits. This preserves the existing forecast event. Transform
each saved draw separately for distribution scores and means. Recomputing
interpolated quantiles after the nonlinear transform is not the same convention
and is unnecessary for this release. No clipping, new tail family or extra
posterior draw generation enters the primary calculations.

4. Freeze one compact input bundle and use one case registry

N00 must bind source hashes and selected identities, then copy the needed small
inputs into a new immutable bundle. Verify hashes before and after copying. An
actively changing input is a stop condition for that input, not permission to
replace it with another run. Continue independent reporting work while resolving
any required input problem.

The canonical dimensions are:
- 14 methods and 70 method-origin representations;
- 140 unique observation cases;
- 1,860 available forecast cases without a scale column;
- 3,720 score and interval rows for two scales;
- 26,040 targeted-quantile rows for seven probabilities and two scales;
- 270 origin/horizon summary rows for two scales.

All methods have eight-day comparisons; 13 have 28-day comparisons. NWS has no
forecast rows at leads 9-28. That is structural unavailability, not a partially
missing 28-day forecast. The current inputs are complete; unexpected missing
members, nonfinite values, wrong dates, duplicate keys or changed counts fail
validation. Do not impute, silently subset, or reduce a comparison denominator.
Ties/duplicate predictions retain their original weights.

Record strict hits y<q, weak hits y<=q, and exact ties y==q; use no numerical
tolerance to turn nearby observations into ties. A closed 95% interval uses
y<lower, lower<=y<=upper, y>upper as an exact partition. Include counts, widths
and interval scores. Discrete forecasts need not have both strict and weak hit
rates equal to the nominal probability. Five origins do not justify treating
every day as an independent replicate for significance claims.

N05 builds this registry once. N06-N08 are logical products of a single
deterministic pass through it, rather than three separate resimulation or
postprocessing campaigns. Keep per-case outputs unrounded. Round only display
tables and derive all summaries/skill ratios from the unrounded case records.
