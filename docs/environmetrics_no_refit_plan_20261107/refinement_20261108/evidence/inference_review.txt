4. N13 source-precision gate: all conditions required before interpretation

G13.1 Provenance:bind the exact selected input, generating source snapshot, audit exporter source, CSV hash, run/quantile identity and generation timestamp/phase. A path name or a manifest commit alone is not proof that a current local formula reproduces a saved computation.
G13.2 Source map:derive the historical-channel rows and forecast-list order from their constructors and saved labels. Verify the exact mapping at the Gaussian pseudo-observation construction, augmentation updates, parameter updates, sampler/exporter, and saved forecast block. No assumed+1 shift or posthoc relabeling passes this test.
G13.3 Update phase:establish which iteration/state update the saved QQQ entries represent and whether saved augmentation/channel moments come from that same phase. Terminal moments can be newer than the last state update or refreshed during sampling. Therefore a mismatch between QQQ and moments does not alone identify a formula/index defect. Comparisons must use phase-matched values, or restrict interpretation to directly stored applied precision values with proven observation labels.
G13.4 Array semantics:for each accepted source/quantile/lead, member IDs and dimensions match the actual forecast matrix and retained output; every included conditional variance is positive/finite. Check duplicates, missing cells, source availability boundaries, and quantile IDs. The representative expected complete layout is7quantiles*(51*28+7*8)members/leads for each quantity. Do not quietly omit failed cells.
G13.5 Mathematical meaning:define P_j,k=sum_i 1/Q_j,i,k for the stored conditional Gaussian pseudo-observation variance. It is conditional observation information in that recorded update, not a posterior mixture weight, marginal influence measure, independent sample count, or general learned source reliability.
G13.6 Scope:report only the representative run if that is the only contract-complete export. Do not extrapolate its precision allocation to all5origins or all model variants.

If anyG13 condition fails or remains unknown, outcome=OPTIONAL_SKIPPED_SOURCE_CONTRACT; include the source hashes and precise unresolved condition in companion evidence only; do not publish numerical precision shares. Do not edit the model's source indexing or overwrite forecasts in this phase. Any confirmed change needed in the inferential calculation receives a separate later-experiment identity. Current numerical results remain frozen, and this gate does not manufacture a historical-error or no-error claim.

5. N12 target-coherence gate: a mathematical audit is not a sampler validation

Prepare an explicit table mapping the displayed model to sampling conditionals:
- Observed gauge channels, retrospective channels and every forecast member/lead; complete active observation index set and counts.
- Historical and forecast state coordinates, selectors, transitions, start/end dimensions, and proper initial distribution.
- Each source's scale/skewness priors, support, transformed-coordinate Jacobians and augmented latent variables.
- All uncertain evolution covariances and their prior/update policy; any deterministic or data-adaptive discount/evolution construction must be stated consistently.
- Conditioning/updated unknowns for each proposed step, including complete forecast-member multiplicities and forecast latent updates.
- Gamma proposals/acceptance ratio and latent-state trajectory draw, rather than a list of incomplete conditional labels.

For any corrected displayed kernel, verify its log density against the joint target algebraically or with a small deterministic conditional-density check under fixed legal inputs. Such checks may be future implementation tests, but no empirical posterior run is authorized. A correct kernel is necessary, not sufficient for a valid ergodic chain or accurate approximation.

Allowed outcomes:
FORMULATION_SCOPE_VERIFIED:all advertised conditionals match the clearly stated target or explicitly delimited subproblem; no performance claim.
FORMULATION_SCOPED_WITH_OPEN_STEPS:retain only proved statements and label their limited scope; future complete sampling construction/implementation remains separate.
FORMULATION_REQUIRES_TARGET_DECISION:defer unresolved blocks; no assertion of an executable full-model MCMC algorithm or empirical VB accuracy.

All three can coexist with a completed no-refit editorial phase if the article states only the corresponding supported scope. The reviewer ledger must retain EMPIRICAL_COMPARISON_REQUIRES_NEW_POSTERIOR_RUN forC7. An empty comparison table or anticipated result must never enter the article.

Future experiment contract minimum:prespecified origin/quantile and complete state dimension (34for the representative model,not31);same data/priors/likelihood and unknowns; sampler correctness evidence;multiple dispersed chains;rankRhat,bulk/tailESS andMCSE, including interval endpoints;means,SD ratios andquantiles for prespecified marginals;runtime/memory;clearly delimited full-history versus reduced-history experiment. Matching only inputs while fixing unknown quantities at VB estimates does not establish a same-target comparison.
